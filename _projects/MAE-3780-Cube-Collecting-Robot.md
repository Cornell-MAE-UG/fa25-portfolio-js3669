For the final project in MAE 3780 (Mechatronics), the objective was to design, build, and program a robot capable of collecting cubes and competing against other robots using an Arduino, color sensors, and QTI sensors. My initial focus was on the wiring and circuitry of the robot, including integrating the Arduino with the various sensors. Since the group worked in a highly collaborative manner, I also contributed to many other aspects of the project, including CAD design, physical assembly of the robot, troubleshooting the code and path logic, and testing and iterating the overall design. Working with Arduino hardware and sensors was a new experience for me, and through this project I developed useful skills in electronics integration, debugging, and iterative engineering design.
